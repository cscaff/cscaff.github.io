I am a graduate research assistant in the Formal Methods & Reasoning Group, where I am advised by Prof. Mark Santolucito. My research is broadly focused on the intersection of formal methods and AI. I am currently working on agentic systems for FPGA programming with a focus on SAT solving. Before starting my graduate studies, I interned at the Georgia Tech Research Institute ARCAID, working at the intersection of ontology and program analysis. I also worked as an undergraduate research programmer at the GATAS Laboratory, working on applied category theory software for scientific computing. You can find me on GitHub, Google Scholar, and LinkedIn.
